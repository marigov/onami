# Privacy Policy for Onami

Effective date: March 9, 2026

Onami ("we", "our", or "us") provides access to radio stations and related listening features. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be collected when you use the app, how it is used, and your choices.

## 1. Information We Collect

### Information stored on your device
Onami stores certain app data locally on your device, such as:
- Favorite stations
- Filter selections, including country, region, language, and genre preferences
- App settings and playback-related preferences
- Purchase status needed to unlock premium features

### Information processed through third-party services
Onami uses third-party services that may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies, including:
- Google Mobile Ads / User Messaging Platform, for advertising and consent management
- Apple, for in-app purchases through StoreKit
- Apple Music, if you choose to open or add recognized songs to Apple Music
- ShazamKit, when song recognition features are used
- Radio stream providers, when you play live radio stations

Depending on how you use the app, these services may process data such as:
- Device and app information
- Approximate location inferred from IP address
- Advertising-related identifiers or consent signals
- Purchase and entitlement information
- Streaming requests needed to play radio content

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Provide radio playback and station discovery features
- Save your favorites and preferences
- Enable premium purchases and restore purchases
- Show advertising in the free version of the app
- Provide song recognition and optional Apple Music actions
- Improve app reliability and functionality

## 3. Advertising

The free version of Onami may display ads. Advertising partners may collect or process data such as device information, identifiers, and consent preferences to provide and measure ads, subject to applicable law and platform rules.

## 4. In-App Purchases

Onami offers premium features through Apple's in-app purchase system. Purchase transactions are processed by Apple, not by us directly.

## 5. Song Recognition and Apple Music

If you use song recognition features, audio samples may be processed through Apple's Shazam technologies to identify tracks. If you choose to open or add a song in Apple Music, Apple may process that request according to its own terms and privacy policy.

## 6. Data Sharing

We do not create user accounts for Onami. We may share data only as needed to operate app features through service providers and platform services, including advertising, streaming, purchase, and song recognition services.

## 7. Data Retention

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you remove the app or clear the related app data. Third-party services may retain data according to their own policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Onami is not directed to children. Because the app provides access to live third-party radio content and advertising, parental discretion is advised.

## 9. Your Choices

You can limit or manage certain data use by:
- Adjusting privacy and tracking settings on your Apple device
- Using consent choices presented in the app, where available
- Avoiding optional features such as Apple Music actions or premium purchases
- Removing the app to delete locally stored app data from your device

## 10. Third-Party Privacy Policies

Third-party services used by Onami may have their own privacy policies. You should review those policies for more information:
- Apple: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted at this URL with a revised effective date.
